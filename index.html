One quick thing

This game is for players aged 18 and over. When were you born?

How it works

• Free to play. All naira, units and tokens in the game are pretend money. Nothing can be cashed out.

• Every new life is a random roll. A Net Baby starts rich and well connected. A Local Baby starts at the bottom, sometimes in debt.

• Keep your meter topped up, your phone charged and your neighbours on your side. Light can go at any time.

• NextPawa (units and data) and Verispon (escrow) are in the game and use game money. The real apps are separate and optional, and you never need them to win.

• Use a nickname anywhere public.

• Generator fumes can kill. They contain carbon monoxide, which you can't see or smell. Never run a generator inside a house, shop or garage, or near doors and windows.
