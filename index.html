• The game is free to play.
• All prices, naira, meter units and tokens are simulated & virtual.

• Use a nickname anywhere public.
• Never do dangerous electrical repairs or run generators indoors/unsafe — in-game or real life.
• Generators must stay outdoors, away from windows. The game enforces this.
